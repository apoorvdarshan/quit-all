Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 22, 2026

Overview

Quit All ("we", "our", "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

Data Collection

Quit All does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All your data stays on your device.

  • Addiction tracking data — stored locally on your device using CoreData
  • Craving logs, relapse history, milestones — stored locally, never transmitted
  • No accounts — no sign-up, no login, no email required
  • No analytics — we don't track your behavior
  • No advertising — we don't serve ads or share data with advertisers

Third-Party Services

  • Giphy API — Used to fetch GIF images for the SOS craving screen. Giphy may collect usage data per their own privacy policy. No personal data from Quit All is sent to Giphy.
  • Apple StoreKit — Used for in-app subscriptions. Payment processing is handled entirely by Apple.

Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device. If you delete the app, all data is permanently deleted. We have no backups of your data.

Children's Privacy

Quit All is not intended for children under 17. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

FAQ

Is my data private?

Yes. All data is stored locally on your device. We never see, collect, or transmit your data.

Can I track multiple addictions?

Yes. You can track all 9 supported addiction types simultaneously, each with its own streak, cost tracking, and craving history.

What happens if I relapse?

No judgment. The app has a compassionate relapse flow that helps you understand what happened and restart your streak. Your previous streak is saved in your history.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to iPhone Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions > Quit All > Cancel Subscription.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request a refund.
