## Description

    Quit All helps you break multiple habits from one private iPhone app.

    Track smoking, vaping, alcohol, social media, weed, gambling, overspending, caffeine, or any combination of them. Build streaks, log cravings, see patterns, track money saved, and use SOS mode when an urge hits.

    WHAT YOU GET
    - Track multiple habits at once
    - SOS craving mode with Powered by KLIPY GIF loops, offline video loops, and motivational memes
    - 2,790+ curated motivational, consequence, distraction, roast, and relatable meme prompts
    - Streaks, milestones, and progress cards
    - Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets for quick progress checks
    - Savings calculator with daily, monthly, yearly, and long-term projections
    - Craving logs, trigger tracking, mood logging, and pattern insights
    - No-shame relapse logging so you can restart without losing context
    - Smart notifications for higher-risk times
    - Manage, edit, and reorder your habits from a dedicated Habits tab
    - About, support, share, rate, and social links
    - Local-first design with no account required

    PRIVATE BY DESIGN
    - No account or login
    - Habit data is stored on your device
    - No ad tracking
    - No habit data is sold
    - Payment is handled by Apple

    PREMIUM
    Quit All Premium unlocks the full app experience, including SOS craving support, stats, milestones, savings projections, widgets, smart notifications, GIF/video loops, and relapse tracking.

    Premium options:
    - Weekly subscription with a 7-day free trial when eligible
    - Yearly subscription with a 7-day free trial when eligible
    - Lifetime one-time purchase

    Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel anytime from Apple ID settings.

    IMPORTANT
    Quit All is a self-help and habit tracking tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. If you are dealing with substance dependence, withdrawal risk, or a medical emergency, contact a qualified healthcare professional or local emergency services.

## Reviewer Notes

    Quit All does not require an account or login. User habit data is stored locally on device with CoreData.

    To test:
    1. Launch the app and complete onboarding.
    2. Choose one or more habits to track.
    3. Test the paywall from onboarding or from app launch after onboarding.
    4. After purchase, test Home, SOS, Stats, Habits, Cost, Milestones, Relapse, Settings, About, and the Home Screen or Lock Screen widget.

    In-app purchases:
    - Weekly: com.quitall.premium.weekly (7-day free trial)
    - Yearly: com.quitall.premium.yearly (7-day free trial)
    - Lifetime: com.quitall.premium.lifetime

    Purchases are processed by Apple and entitlement state is managed through RevenueCat. Subscriptions can be tested with Apple sandbox/TestFlight.

    The app includes GIF/video motivational content for craving distraction and habit support. Online KLIPY GIF content shows Powered by KLIPY attribution near the media. It is not a medical app and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
